## Featured project

### AISecretary — in active development

A multi-tenant B2B SaaS platform that gives small and medium businesses an AI receptionist. It answers customer questions in several languages, books appointments, and syncs them to Google Calendar.

- **Modular by design:** 11 capability modules (appointments, reservations, CRM, billing, inventory and more), switched on per business
- **Real actions, not just chat:** the AI calls backend tools that check live availability and create bookings
- **Built for operators:** subscription billing (Stripe, iyzico), analytics, selectable themes, and per-business pricing and policies
- **Knowledge base:** FAQ and quick-info context today, with a document-based RAG pipeline (pgvector, Celery, Redis) in progress
- **Next:** Telegram, WhatsApp and Instagram channels, and an optional owner-approval step for AI-made bookings

`Django` · `DRF` · `PostgreSQL` · `Celery` · `Redis` · `Docker` · `React` · `TypeScript`
